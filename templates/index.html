Vehicle Identifier

Welcome! This tool uses smart image recognition to instantly detect and classify different types of transportation from your photos.

Please note that this app only supports these transportation types such as bikes, cars, motorcycles, auto rickshaws, planes, ships, and trains.

How to use it: Simply upload a clear picture of one of these supported vehicles from your device and click the button below. Our system will analyze the photo and show you its best guess, along with a confidence score showing how sure it is about the result.
